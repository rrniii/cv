## Research Contributions and Impact

-  | **New polar-cloud observables**: conceived, built and deployed [CAPABL](https://doi.org/10.1175/JTECH-D-12-00101.1), making ice-crystal orientation directly observable. Supervised and co-led studies that established its [radiative influence](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018JD028963) over the Greenland Ice Sheet.
-  | **Cloud-process tests**: directed ICECAPS-ACE as UK PI, establishing observations of aerosol-limited cloud regimes and fog--aerosol interactions. Contributed Summit measurements and interpretation to [idealised simulations testing mixed-phase-cloud dissipation](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-22-8973-2022).
-  | **Climate-model development**: identified inconsistent treatments of stratospheric aerosol in radiation and chemistry and implemented a [consistent prescription in CESM1](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-9-2459-2016), improving the model's response to volcanic forcing.
-  | **New ecological evidence from weather radar**: led the research as senior author on [BioDAR's national-scale assessment of aerial arthropod abundance](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.70425), turning biological echoes into observations of aerial biodiversity.
-  | **Operational radar impact**: led Environment Agency and SEPA partnerships developing quality-controlled rainfall products and methods for national radar-composite and flood-modelling workflows; connects research observations with operational environmental practice.
