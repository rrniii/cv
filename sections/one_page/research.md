## Research and Leadership

-  | **Polar-cloud observations**: conceived, built and deployed CAPABL at Summit, Greenland, making ice-crystal orientation directly observable; supervised and co-led studies of its influence on surface radiation. UK PI for ICECAPS-ACE and ICECAPS-MELT.
-  | **National radar capability**: developed and leads NCAS mobile weather- and cloud-radar facilities. As WOEST PI, designed and coordinated a six-organisation observing campaign to study convective turbulence and test forecast-model assumptions.
-  | **Polar observatories**: co-PI of GAMB2LE, developing an autonomous, sustainably powered Greenland observatory with Heather Guy and Sarah Barr; co-established the Troll cloud observatory and leads SnøTroll in Antarctica.
-  | **Radar aeroecology**: radar lead and senior author of BioDAR's national-scale assessment of aerial arthropod abundance, using eight years of observations from 15 UK weather radars.
